## Dev log

### API-5109 — Supplier order-flow fixes (invoice dedup, price soft-fail, terminal completion, reconcile, activity subtypes)

*Repo:* `master_service` · *Branch:* `API-5109` · *Ticket:* [API-5109 — General Fixed And Improvements with Order Flow](https://linear.app/zuperinc/issue/API-5109) · *Commits:* `1a75a3093`

- Stopped duplicate supplier invoices from being attached: an Invoice Update now records the invoice number + date once on the order's INVOICED milestone (works even when there's no invoice PDF, e.g. ABC) and skips repeats; the PDF attachment stays separate and safe to retry.
- Sending an order to the supplier no longer blocks when a line can't get a fresh live price — it goes out on the last-known price, records where that price came from, and the reconcile pass corrects it once the supplier order comes back. Only a total price-service failure or a genuinely unknown item stops the send.
- Moving a purchase order to a terminal status (Fulfilled / Work Completed / Closed) now tells the external order tracker the supplier order is complete — this rescues pickup ("will call") orders that never send a delivery webhook and would otherwise sit open forever.
- Reviewed the reconcile flow: kept it from ever re-pricing a line across a different pricing unit (which could badly distort the price), and made the "couldn't reconcile" messages say *why* (different price UOM vs item not on the order). Fixed an ABC case where a zero-length item produced a bad internal product id and broke matching.
- Added activity sub-events: the supplier-update activity can collapse into a single timeline entry with the individual changes nested underneath, behind an off-by-default flag until the PO activity feed can display them.
- Open item flagged on the ticket: needs platform confirmation on which event id the tracker's completion endpoint expects and whether it re-processes the event.
