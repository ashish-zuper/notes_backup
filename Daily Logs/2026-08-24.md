## Dev log

### API-5572 — QXO order templates and product import
*Repo:* `master_service` · *Branch:* `API-5572` · *Ticket:* [API-5572 — QXO Templates Worker functions](https://linear.app/zuperinc/issue/API-5572/qxo-templates-worker-functions) · *Commits:* `fd1ca33a2`

- QXO order templates can now be browsed and imported. Listing is a single paginated supplier call; importing a template pulls its products, prices them per branch and creates the vendor catalog rows.
- Prices are requested per SKU in the unit each product is cataloged in. QXO will not quote a price in a bulk packaging unit, and the supplier's raw unit list puts one first, so unit lists now lead with the inventory unit — without this most products imported at price 0.
- QXO is now treated as branch-scoped, like ABC Supply. That enables the vendor branch filter and the branch-availability lookup, both of which previously returned "not supported" for QXO.
- A template's account is stored alongside its supplier template id, because the supplier refuses a template detail request without one. Re-imports read it locally instead of looking it up again.
- Added searchable logging across the import, including one line per pricing request showing what was asked for and how much came back priced — the signal that was missing when prices silently came back empty.
