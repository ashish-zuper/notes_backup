## Dev log

### API-5562 — QXO purchase order submit (phase 1)

*Repo:* `master_service` · *Branch:* `API-5562` · *Ticket:* [API-5562 — Master Service: Setup QXO : Material Order Setup](https://linear.app/zuperinc/issue/API-5562/master-service-setup-qxo-material-order-setup) · *Commits:* `b1b76338b`

- Purchase orders on a QXO-linked vendor can now be placed over QXO's API when moved to Sent to Vendor, instead of failing — QXO joins SRS and ABC Supply on the integrated-supplier path.
- Prices are refreshed from QXO immediately before submitting and written back onto the PO. A QXO pricing outage no longer blocks the order; it goes out on last-known prices with a warning on the PO instead.
- Availability is checked live by QXO at submit rather than against our nightly stock snapshot.
- The PO stays at Sent to Vendor after a successful submit. QXO publishes no webhooks, so order confirmation, delivery and invoicing wait on the phase 2 poller.
