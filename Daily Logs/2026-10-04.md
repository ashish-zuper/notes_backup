## Dev log

### API-6145 — Vendor re-cost keeps saved cost on lines without a catalog row

*Repo:* `master_service` · *Branch:* `API-6145` · *Ticket:* [API-6145 — Vendor re-cost applies master cost to lines without a vendor catalog](https://linear.app/zuperinc/issue/API-6145/vendor-re-cost-applies-master-cost-to-lines-without-a-vendor-catalog) · *Commits:* `294867db1`

- Fetching a package with a cost vendor re-costed line items to the product's master purchase price whenever that vendor had no catalog rows for any product in the package. Lines now keep their saved cost and price unless the vendor has a catalog cost for that product. Covers invoice/estimate packages, proposal template option packages and service contract packages.
- Bundle components follow the same rule. A component with a vendor catalog cost is now re-costed even when its product has no master cost, and its old purchase price is the line's saved cost rather than the product's current cost.
- Proposal template rollup bundles no longer swap their saved cost for the sum of component master costs when no component received a vendor cost.

### API-5948 — QXO catalog shows "Call for availability" instead of "Unavailable"

*Repo:* `master_service`, `scheduler_service` · *Branch:* `API-5948` · *Ticket:* [API-5948 — QXO - Observation - Bug bash](https://linear.app/zuperinc/issue/API-5948/qxo-observation-bug-bash) · *Commits:* `7562ca950` `79d7ae7`

- QXO vendor catalog rows not stocked at their branch now read "Call for availability" instead of "Unavailable". QXO has no in-stock flag, so it can't actually confirm an item is out of stock.
- Applied everywhere QXO availability is written: the nightly price sync, catalog import/re-pricing, and the on-demand availability refresh. SRS and ABC still show "Unavailable".
- Existing QXO rows switch over on their next nightly sync.
