## Dev log

### API-4763 — ABC Supply order submission + tracking

*Repo:* `master_service` · *Branch:* `API-4763` · *Ticket:* [API-4763 — PO Related Changes for ABC](https://linear.app/zuperinc/issue/API-4763/po-related-changes-for-abc) · *Commits:* `4fed2e9ef`

- On "Send to Vendor", a purchase order for an ABC Supply vendor is now placed as a real order in ABC Supply (bringing ABC to parity with the existing SRS flow). ABC returns a confirmation number, which is stored on the PO and surfaced in its activity timeline.
- Order tracking rides ABC's order-update events: the PO advances to vendor-approved, line prices/quantities and the delivery date reconcile against ABC's order, and delivery progress (in transit → arrived → delivered, including pickup orders) is recorded on the PO.
- Handles ABC's per-order-type status vocabulary and multi-line pricing; requested and delivered dates are interpreted in the company timezone so they land on the intended calendar day. Availability is recorded at price time but does not block submission.
- Invoice-PDF retrieval is scaffolded but intentionally disabled until ABC enables the invoice endpoint for the integration.
