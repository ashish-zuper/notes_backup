## Dev log

### API-6268 — Supplier adoption metrics
*Repo:* `zuper_metrics` · *Branch:* `api-6268-track-supplier-integration-adoption-in-zuper_metrics` · *Ticket:* [API-6268 — Track supplier integration adoption in zuper_metrics (supplier_stats)](https://linear.app/zuperinc/issue/API-6268/track-supplier-integration-adoption-in-zuper-metrics-supplier-stats) · *Commits:* `29945d9` `25fccff`

- Added a `supplier_stats` table: one row per company and supplier type (SRS / ABC Supply / QXO, plus NONE for non-supplier vendors), so supplier adoption can be tracked in Metabase.
- Tracks supplier vendors and products, quotes using a supplier vendor and how many led to a PO, PO counts and value with and without a linked quote, and POs actually placed with, failed at, or delivered by the supplier.
- Only covers companies with supplier integration enabled or a supplier connected, computed with a few queries per company batch so the nightly job doesn't get slower per company.
- DDL is in `migrations.md` and must be run in each region before the job ships.
- Failed supplier submissions only count once the PO has been untouched for 24h, so SRS orders still waiting in the supplier's queue aren't reported as failures.
