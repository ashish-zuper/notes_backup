When a new job is created under Medicare job category, create a child job of category Medicaid
	- job title = medicare job title + "Medicaid"
	- customer = medicare customer (if exists)
	- organization = medicare org (if exists)
	- Copy custom field value of Hubspot Ticket Id from Medicare job to the Medicaid job
	- copy line items (products) if any

When the Medicaid job status is moved to Closed, copy the value of custom field: Medical History to the associated Parent job (Medicare category) custom field: Medical History
