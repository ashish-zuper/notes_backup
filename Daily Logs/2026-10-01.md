## Dev log

### API-6041 — SRS import builds products from the stored master doc

*Repo:* `master_service`, `scheduler_service` · *Branch:* `API-6041` · *Ticket:* [API-6041 — SRS: build import catalog product from stored master doc, not product_json](https://linear.app/zuperinc/issue/API-6041/srs-build-import-catalog-product-from-stored-master-doc-not-product) · *Commits:* `2b0932fa2` (master_service) `9c5f03b` (scheduler_service)

- SRS template imports now build catalog products from the stored master product fields, like ABC and QXO, so variants carry their per-branch availability; older docs without an order unit still fall back to the raw SRS payload.
- The nightly SRS import now stores the product description and each variant's order unit, which the stored-doc build needs.
- Fixed SRS (and likely QXO) products fetched live on their first import getting no catalog rows: they were saved with an empty branch list, which reads as "stocked nowhere". Unsynced products now have no branch list until the branch sync runs, and the worker no longer writes a stale list over a concurrent sync.
- Option values sent to SRS for pricing are kept exactly as the nightly price sync sends them, so price-to-variant matching is unchanged.

### API-6097 — QXO delivery photos as proof of delivery

*Repo:* `master_service` · *Branch:* `API-6097` · *Ticket:* [API-6097 — QXO Delivery Photos: Link proof of delivery photos to Material Order](https://linear.app/zuperinc/issue/API-6097/qxo-delivery-photos-link-proof-of-delivery-photos-to-material-order) · *Commits:* `34b6e00c0`

- QXO's delivery photos are now attached to the material order as its proof of delivery, through the same flow SRS uses for its proof-of-delivery PDF. They appear in the order's files, and on the Fulfilled status entry when one exists.
- Photos link to QXO's public URLs instead of being copied to S3.
- The QXO order poll keeps checking delivered orders and links photos QXO adds later, until the order is invoiced or ages out. A photo already on the order is never added twice.
- Delivered QXO orders no longer get a misleading "proof of delivery could not be retrieved" activity, and a failing attach is retried at most once a day so it can't flood the order's activity.

### API-6112 — Supplier import and price sync follow-ups

*Repo:* `scheduler_service` · *Branch:* `API-6112` · *Ticket:* [API-6112 — Further enhancements](https://linear.app/zuperinc/issue/API-6112/further-enhancements) · *Commits:* `3316b56` `04ad8c3`

- The nightly price sync started at 00:00 alongside the supplier import in the same process, which pushed production's nightly peak to 931 MB. It now runs at 00:05 and, while an import is running, retries every 15 minutes, running anyway after 2 hours. Restarts during the wait no longer skip the night's run, and manual per-company syncs still start immediately and no longer merge into each other or into a full run.
- SRS pricing claimed a limit of 5 requests at a time but actually fired every request for a company at once and kept every response in memory. It now runs 5 at a time with a short pause between batches, and catalogs that share a product key share one request.
- The SRS token is now shared across pricing and availability, fetched only when an SRS supplier is priced and refreshed once on expiry, so an SRS login failure no longer blocks ABC and QXO pricing.
