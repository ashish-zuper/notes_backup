### API-6420 — Supplier tracker completion on fulfilled and paid POs
*Repo:* `master_service` · *Branch:* `api-6420-mark-supplier-order-complete-on-tracker-for-fulfilled-and` · *Ticket:* [API-6420 — Mark supplier order complete on tracker for fulfilled and paid POs](https://linear.app/zuperinc/issue/API-6420/mark-supplier-order-complete-on-tracker-for-fulfilled-and-paid-pos) · *Commits:* `92a61d464`
- Fixed supplier POs not being marked Completed on the supplier order tracker when fulfilled by receiving items, by editing line items, or through bulk status updates; before, only a direct status change to Fulfilled marked it.
