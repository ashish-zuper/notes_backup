### API-4710 — Vendor catalog list 16MB overflow fix
*Repo:* `master_service` · *Branch:* `API-4710` · *Commits:* `7b3e47b40`

- Fixed the vendor catalogs list API (`group_by=VENDOR`) returning a 500 for variant-heavy products. The grouped query embedded a full copy of the vendor and product into every catalog row, so products with 180+ catalogs per vendor exceeded MongoDB's 16MB per-document limit.
- The VENDOR-grouped response now carries only the per-row line-item fields the UI actually uses; the per-row vendor was a duplicate of the group-level vendor and the product was unused. Product-grouped responses are left as-is for now.
