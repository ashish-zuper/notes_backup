## Overview

This workflow automatically notifies customers via SMS and email whenever a new job is created under the "Roof Inspection" category. The notification prompts the customer to schedule the inspection job at their convenience.

---

## Configurable Parameters

| Parameter            | Type      | Description                                                      | Example Value                |
|----------------------|-----------|------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------|
| `email_subject`      | static    | Subject for the email notification                               | `Schedule Your Job`          |
| `email_body`         | dynamic   | Email content with job and scheduling details                    | Generated by workflow        |
| `sms_body`           | dynamic   | SMS content with job and scheduling details                      | Generated by workflow        |

---

## How Parameters Are Used

- **Trigger Condition:**  
  The workflow is triggered when a new job is created with the category "Roof Inspection" and a valid customer UID.

- **Job Details Fetch:**  
  Retrieves job and customer details, including the public scheduling link.

- **Notification Payload Generation:**  
  Generates personalized email and SMS content using job and customer information.

- **Notification Delivery:**  
  Sends the generated email and SMS to the customer using Zuper's notification system.

---

## Process Flow

1. **Trigger:**  
   The workflow starts when a new job is created under the "Roof Inspection" category with a valid customer.

2. **Fetch Job Details:**  
   Retrieves job and customer details, including the scheduling link.

3. **Generate Notification Payload:**  
   Uses job and customer data to create personalized email and SMS content.

4. **Split:**  
   Splits the workflow to send both email and SMS notifications in parallel.

5. **Send Email Notification:**  
   Sends an email to the customer with scheduling instructions and job details.

6. **Send SMS Notification:**  
   Sends an SMS to the customer with scheduling instructions and job details.
